Sacred Nava Naga Kshetram

Sri Oshadheeshwara Nava Naga Subrahmanyeshwara Swamy Devalayam, nestled in Mandal, Kyathampalle, village, Dharmasagar, Telangana 506151 Telangana, is a divine abode dedicated to the nine celestial serpents (Nava Nagas) and Lord Subrahmanyeshwara. This sacred kshetram is revered for Sarpa Dosha nivarana, bestowing prosperity, progeny, and protection from Rahu-Ketu afflictions.

Ananta Naga

Eternal cosmic serpent, bestows stability

Vasuki Naga

King of serpents, removes obstacles

Subrahmanyeshwara

Lord of serpents & wisdom, grants victory

"Worshipping the Nava Nagas here liberates from kala sarpa dosha" – Chief Priest Sri Sita Ramaya
